at the commit above plus its uncommitted changes: a new blocking scan layer
installed into the pre-commit, pre-merge-commit and pre-applypatch hooks; a new

skills and workflow collection.

blobs into a temporary directory to diffing the index directly. This report
evaluates the final design, confirmed unchanged across repeated checks before
