# Vietnamese register and meaning

Use formal finance prose. Translate the source hedge rather than strengthening a forecast. `May rise` can be `có thể tăng`; `is expected to rise` can be `dự kiến tăng`. Do not turn either into a certain increase.

Preserve relative positions. `Overweight technology by 3 percentage points` describes a weight above the benchmark; it is not an instruction to increase holdings. Translate as `Tỷ trọng ngành công nghệ trong danh mục cao hơn chỉ số tham chiếu 3 điểm phần trăm.`

Keep Latin financial identifiers such as ROE, EPS, EBITDA, P/E, P/FCF and VaR. A first-use Vietnamese gloss helps readers; subsequent identifiers need no repeated gloss. Named factor scores retain their English identifiers and exact values.

Use Vietnamese diacritics. Sentence length is a readability review: split long independent clauses without dropping qualifications or changing attribution. Source paragraph boundaries remain intact. Keep company identity and listing ticker distinct from a translated display name.

Retail language can explain technical terms once. Rating labels retain both the product rating and Vietnamese label. Language choice does not authorize a different distribution posture.
